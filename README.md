# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

An end-to-end data analytics project analyzing retail customer purchasing patterns to identify trends, segment customers, and provide actionable business recommendations.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a retail company's customer behavior dataset to answer the key business question: **"How can the company leverage consumer shopping data to identify trends, improve customer engagement, and optimize marketing and product strategies?"**

### Key Insights Delivered

- Customer segmentation by loyalty (new, returning, loyal based on purchase frequency)
- Revenue and sales analysis across demographics, product categories, and channels
- Discount sensitivity and spending patterns
- Subscription impact on customer lifetime value
- Age group and gender purchasing behavior

## Tools & Technologies

- **Python (pandas)**: Data cleaning and transformation
- **SQL**: Data analysis and customer segmentation queries
- **Power BI**: Interactive dashboard for stakeholder insights

## Key Analysis Questions Answered

1. What is the total revenue generated by male vs. female customers?
2. Which customers used a discount but still spent more than the average purchase amount?
3. Which are the top 5 products with the highest average review rating?
4. Do subscribed customers spend more? Compare average spend and total revenue between subscribers and non-subscribers.
5. What is the revenue contribution of each age group?
6. Which products have the highest percentage of purchases with discounts applied?
7. How do customer segments (New, Returning, Loyal) differ in count and behavior?

## Key Findings

- **73% of customers are non-subscribers**, representing a significant conversion opportunity
- **Young adults generate the highest revenue** across all age groups
- **Clothing category dominates** both sales volume and revenue
- **Subscribed customers show higher average spend**, validating the subscription model
- **Discount-sensitive customers still maintain above-average purchase amounts**, suggesting strategic pricing opportunities

## Business Recommendations

1. Launch targeted subscription campaigns for high-frequency purchasers
2. Optimize clothing inventory based on dominant category performance
3. Develop age-specific marketing strategies, particularly for young adult segment
4. Implement tiered discount strategies to maximize revenue while maintaining customer satisfaction
